## Overall Impression

Calm, legible, honest — numbers are the loudest thing on screen, as intended. But it is not yet confident: the best asset (the stack drawing) is buried under a form column, Help is clipped exactly when requested, and the export moment — the product's reason to exist — is flat. Every prior-run P1/P2 is gone; what remains is about emphasis, not correctness.

## What's Working

1. **Honest numbers.** Fit sentences name dimension and limit; per-part fits column; readiness footers state exactly what's true ("You can start assembling before you export").
2. **Filaments-first colour.** The only saturated colour anywhere is the user's own filaments — header dots, swatches, drawer fronts, plate polygons.
3. **Real safety nets and keyboard work.** Undo with named consequences, Esc returns focus from the viewer (verified), arrow-key reorder, visible 2px focus ring, both themes coherent.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Help is squeezed to uselessness when requested.** With a layer open, Help measures 130px tall against 942px of content under a 616px Preview; at 390px it sits ~2168px from the field. The product's "options explain themselves" promise breaks at the moment of use. Fix: give Help first claim on the sticky column (compact the Preview / cap it ~40% while a topic is active), or fly help out beside the field; inline below the field at narrow widths. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`
- **[P1] The export moment has no weight and no summary.** Completion is a note-coloured "done in 12s" plus file rows; nothing summarises printers/plates/time/grams or confirms the files match the current stack, and staleness has no persistent mark. Fix: a result card per export (per printer: plates, time, grams, Download, "matches your current stack") that flips to a caution state when the stack changes, mirrored as a small mark on the Plates tab. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` + `/impeccable delight`
- **[P2] Plates fronts configuration; Design buries the drawing.** ~600px of packing settings precede the first plate; the elevation is a side-column card beside a long form. Fix: plate grid directly under Export, packing controls in a disclosure whose summary shows current values; on Design give the drawing a larger share and let clicking a drawer front open that layer's editor. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Parts is a wall of repetition.** Every table row repeats the role select and "fits every enabled printer", so the one misfit would drown. Fix: quiet check when a part fits, prose only on exception; per-row colour as a swatch button opening a role menu; move "Change a whole group" beside the role rows. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`
- **[P3] Accessibility semantics and target sizes.** No `<main>`/skip link; `role="tab"` without tablist/tabpanel; `?` buttons 18×18px, checkboxes 13×13px; tab numerals at 60% opacity; `window.alert` for JSON errors. Fix: main landmark + skip link, `aria-current="step"` on plain nav buttons (or a real tablist), ≥24px hit areas, full-opacity numerals, inline `role="alert"`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` then `/impeccable harden`

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Opens on "733 STL files indexed" and Depth; nothing defines layer/sustainer/facade until a `?` is clicked — and that click reveals a 130px clipped card, easily read as "Help is broken". Plates leads with packing settings before any plate; export ends at "done in 12s" with no next step.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** No `<main>` or skip link — 8–10 header controls to tab past per step (252px sticky header at 390px). Help content exists in an aria-live region but is visually clipped and not keyboard-scrollable unless focusable.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the stack drawing is the product, why is it a side-column card instead of the surface being edited — what if clicking a drawer front opened its editor and the form became a short inspector?
2. What should the user feel when the 3MFs exist: a receipt, a slicer checklist, or a persistent "ready" state that survives until the stack changes?
3. Would Parts be easier to trust if it showed only exceptions (does not fit, file missing, colour overridden) and collapsed everything fine into one line?
