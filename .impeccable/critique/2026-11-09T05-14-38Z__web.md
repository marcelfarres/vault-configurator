## Overall Impression

A disciplined, honest planner whose numbers can be trusted and whose Parts screen genuinely shows the real thing — held back by silent failure at its highest-stakes moment (export) and an editing loop scattered across a very long page. Biggest opportunity: make readiness loud — a verdict before export — since the brief itself says a wrong number should be the loudest thing on screen, and right now failure is the quietest.

## What's Working

1. **Parts is the strongest screen.** Role rows tie each filament select to chips of affected parts; the piece grid shows swatch/name/role/qty; swatches flip live on filament change — "show the real thing" made visible.
2. **Fit language is honest where it appears.** "· does not fit" on layer rows, "2U · 99.5 mm" option labels, mono bed sizes on export chips — trust-the-numbers at control level.
3. **Quiet, disciplined system.** Hairlines over shadows, one teal for selection/state, 2.25rem rhythm, tokenised dark mode, stable scrollbar gutter. The StepFooter ("what's true now, one way onward, never a gate") with focus moved to the next H1 is a smart pattern.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Export and preview fail silently when files are missing**
- **What:** With no archive, Export 3MF / Preview plates show "done in 0s" / nothing, footer "0 plates planned · nothing exported yet", primary button inked and enabled. `/api/plan` returned 0 plates for 26 part rows; no reason surfaces.
- **Why it matters:** The product's high-stakes moment reads as broken or as success. Violates "Trust the numbers" and "when something cannot be printed, say so".
- **Fix:** Pre-export readiness summary (N parts, M missing files); if any missing, a Fault-washed full-width line ("26 parts need files from your archive. Nothing can be exported yet.") linking to the archive instructions; demote/disable Export until ≥1 plate; Preview always lands on a visible result or the reason it's empty.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`, then `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P1] The editing loop is split across the page**
- **What:** A layer editor expands ~350px inline; the Help card it feeds sits under Preview in the right column — after clicking Facades, ~7px of Help was visible in an 800px viewport. Preview is not sticky. Design runs 3251px for 2 layers.
- **Why it matters:** The feature that teaches the designer's rules is out of sight at the moment of use; the drawing can't be watched while editing.
- **Fix:** Make the right column sticky with its own scroll; or move Help inline under the focused field / inside the open editor. Keep the stack preview always visible.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

**[P1] Layer rows tell you nothing, and destructive actions have no safety**
- **What:** Rows read "S sustainer" + height pill + ×. Facade split, lock, interior, rails are invisible until opened. × deletes instantly; "Start over" wipes layers/overrides/roof/mods with no confirm — while printer removal has confirm + Undo.
- **Why it matters:** The unit of work can't be scanned; losing a configured layer is one stray click.
- **Fix:** One-line Pencil-Grey summary per row ("S · 2U · 1+1 facades · grid dividers · key"); inline "Layer removed · Undo" reusing the printer pattern; confirm Start over with the layer count; add Duplicate layer.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`, then `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] The header is overloaded and unstructured**
- **What:** One wrapping row holds brand, project name, Save, Load, "Enabled", 4 printer chips, 5 filament dots + count, fit checkbox, fit-target select, Settings. At 1200px it wraps and strands Settings; at 390px it's 210px tall and the H1 starts at y=687 — the first screen is all chrome.
- **Why it matters:** Global state with no hierarchy on every tab; "Enabled" has no noun; fit controls act far from where their effect shows.
- **Fix:** Split identity/project row from a labelled context row ("Printers: …", "Fit against: [select]"); move "Only offer choices that fit" next to the choices it filters.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

**[P2] The archive-missing state is too loud and not actionable**
- **What:** The onboarding block pushes the H1 to y=391 and includes an irrelevant handoff note; each layer adds "File not found: …" lines while the stack still reads healthy; no "Check again" after setting the path.
- **Why it matters:** This is the universal first-run state and the front door to the product.
- **Fix:** One sentence + expandable details + Check again button; missing files as one count in footer/Parts (footer already counts them); drop the handoff note from this block.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable onboard`, then `/impeccable clarify`.

## Questions to Consider

- The stack drawing is the most product-specific thing in the app. What if it were the main canvas — large, sticky, click a block to edit, drag to reorder, colour and fit state on the drawing itself — instead of a 220px thumbnail beside a form?
- Export is the whole point, yet it's a small card above five unlabelled settings. What if Plates opened with a verdict ("12 plates · 4.3 kg · 38 h · all parts placed") and settings collapsed into "Layout options"?
- The brief says a wrong number should be the loudest thing on screen. In the empty-archive run nothing is loud: 26 missing files, a zero-plate export and an inked Export button coexist quietly. Is the quiet system too quiet in a failure state?
