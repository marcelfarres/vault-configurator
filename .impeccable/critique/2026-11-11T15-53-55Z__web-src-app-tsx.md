## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Product-specific in workflow, less specific in its most prominent visual. The numbered Design → Parts → Plates → Assemble sequence, filament-role mapping, printer dimensions, facade vocabulary and hardware checklist could not serve an unrelated product unchanged, and the paper/ink/teal system suits a maker's planning bench. But the large Preview looks authoritative while drawing a generic cabinet: choosing 1+1U facades yields a "2 boxes" summary and still one drawer front with one handle. The biggest specificity opportunity is not branding — it is making the drawing earn the trust its size and precision imply.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI `impeccable detect --json web/src` exited 0 with `[]` (22 files), but only regex rules ran; parsed-page rules (contrast, tiny-text) did not, so the zero is an undercount, not a clean bill. The live overlay (8 views: Design ×2, Parts, Plates, Assemble, Settings ×3) found exactly one rule: `nested-cards`, 6 hits on `div.layer-row` (×1) and `div.filament-row` (×5). Both assessments agree these are false positives — bordered list rows inside a section card, tonally recessed, no shadow — and A independently raised no issue with either row pattern. The detector caught nothing real that A missed; A's P1 (Preview fidelity) is beyond detector reach. If the row borders are ever revisited, hairline dividers would silence the rule, but it is not a defect.

## Overall Impression

The Lovable port landed as a coherent, calmer, more spacious shell — the redesign itself is not the problem. What the critique surfaces is mostly inherited product behavior the new prominence exposes: a schematic Preview that reads as a promise, a next-step button buried under an inventory, and printer identity spread across three places. Biggest opportunity: make the Preview faithful (or honestly labelled), because trust is this product's stated principle #1.

## What's Working

1. **The four-step architecture follows the physical job** — compose, colour, plate, assemble — rather than implementation structure.
2. **Colour behaves like information.** Filament swatches and selection states carry the saturation against restrained chrome, in both themes.
3. **Consequential actions are respected:** missing files block export, reset states its consequences, layer removal has Undo, assembly progress scope ("stays in this browser") is stated.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Make the Preview describe the configuration it claims to preview.** After switching Facades 2U → 1+1U, the summary says "2 boxes" but the elevation still shows one drawer front and one handle (`Preview.tsx` draws one rectangle per layer, ignoring `layer.facades`). Why it matters: the drawing is the strongest assurance in the composition loop; users can't catch a wrong facade setup before committing filament and print time. Fix: draw actual bay/facade divisions with a handle per front, mark the edited layer, and keep a visible "schematic" label (plus accessory callouts) until faithful. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish` (Preview fidelity is a build task: implement in `Preview.tsx`, then polish).
- **[P2] Keep the next action beside the finished design.** "Continue to colours" sits below the full "Parts in this stack" inventory, which Parts then repeats. Fix: place "Continue to Parts & colours" directly after Finish with a compact layer/dimension/blocker summary; collapse the Design inventory behind a count. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Give the layer editor an internal hierarchy.** Six selectors, three checkboxes and three operations share one wrapping block; dependencies (split facades → required rails) are not spatially explained. Cognitive load scored 4/8 failures, concentrated here. Fix: group into Structure / Drawers & interior / Details, keep required rails beside the facade choice, move/duplicate on their own row. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill` (then `/impeccable layout`).
- **[P2] Make printer settings readable at desktop width.** At 1440px the Printers table overflows its 64rem column: Profile values clip and the remove action needs horizontal scrolling, while fit/export/packing printer choices must be reconciled across header, Settings and Plates. Fix: widen the Printers content area or use summary rows + expanded editor; size Bed/Z fields to content and give the width to Name/Profile. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Separate the sample from real-build readiness.** The synthetic plate sample is labelled "Not VAULT parts", yet the footer still reports "2 plates planned" and "0/2 plates marked printed" for it. Fix: an explicit "Example plate layout" section speaking of example plates only; suppress printed-progress language for synthetic plans. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** Reaching Parts means scrolling past the duplicated Design inventory; printer selection exists twice (Export-for toggles vs "Pack plates for") and must be reconciled mentally; the Settings table hides profile text behind horizontal scroll at the primary desktop size.

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Picks 1+1U, sees "2 boxes" in text but an unchanged drawing — cannot connect option to physical result. "Continue to colours" vs a tab named "Parts" forces a needless translation. "Point the planner at your VAULT folder" doesn't open a picker; it navigates to prose. Sample plates speak in real progress terms.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** Hardware checkboxes are named bare "Have" (should be "Have 12 M4 × 14 mm bolts"); the fit selector's accessible name is "All printers" — its value, not its purpose ("Judge fit against" exists only as visually-hidden text the name doesn't use); at 390px, Help scrolls away from the field with no "Back to Interior" return. Positives: keyboard layer reorder with spoken instructions, named help buttons, visible focus rings. Not a full screen-reader certification.

## Minor Observations

- The sticky shell costs ~250px of an 844px-tall narrow viewport (secondary: phone use is not a product requirement, but zoomed/narrow windows feel it).
- Filament rows repeat name/material as static text beside the editable name and material controls ("Charcoal" / "Charcoal · PLA" / "PLA") — drop the static label unless a spool adds information.
- Assemble is one long list; per-layer collapse and "hide checked" would speed repeat use.
- The 4.5rem project-name field truncates longer names visually; let it grow within a bound or expand on focus.
- README/`docs/media` screenshots still show the pre-Lovable rendition (noted during documentation; docs drift, not UI).
- `nested-cards` detector hits on layer/filament rows: dismissed as false positives by both assessments.

## Questions to Consider

1. What should the Preview promise — a faithful front elevation, a deliberately labelled schematic, or an archive-backed model? Its prominence currently promises more than its geometry delivers.
2. Where should users commit the design: right after Finish, in a sticky readiness strip, or after a mandatory parts review? The current page does all three halfway.
3. Should sample mode be a separate demonstration, an inline example, or absent once a stack exists?

**Ship note:** nothing here is P0, and the one P1 predates the redesign (the port enlarged the Preview, which raises the stakes). The interim SHIP verdict stands on the evidence — with the Preview fix as the clear first follow-up, since "trust the numbers" is the product's first principle.
