Send your slicer presets to this server

This page runs on a server, which has no slicer of its own. Pick a slicer's settings folder on your computer: its printer, process and filament presets (yours and the built-in ones, JSON files only) are copied here. Pick it again whenever you change a preset in the slicer. Your browser asks to confirm the upload of many files.

Step 3 · Plates & export

One 3MF project per printer, several plates inside, every plate a single filament. Parts of one colour are packed together (MaxRects, 90° rotation allowed); the biggest parts go to the biggest printer.

Step 4 · Tune a process preset per part group (optional)

Tick test on the groups you want to tune, press Export test plates: each printer gets a TEST file with one plate per group holding one of each part (an assembly's parts together). Tune and save a preset in the slicer, press Rescan presets, pick it here; the real export then applies it. Per-part rows override the group.
